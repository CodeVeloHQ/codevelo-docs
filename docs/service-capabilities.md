# Service capabilities

CodeVelo combines software and physical infrastructure services so clients can work with
one accountable technical partner from discovery through ongoing support.

## Web platforms and applications

- Business websites and content-driven platforms
- Web applications, portals, and internal systems
- Frontend and backend development
- Integrations and workflow automation
- Performance, accessibility, and maintainability improvements

## Managed hosting and reliability

- Managed application and website hosting
- Uptime and service monitoring
- Backups, certificates, updates, and maintenance
- Performance and web-quality reporting
- Incident coordination and ongoing support plans

## Commercial networks

- Network and Wi-Fi planning
- Rack and equipment layout
- Switching, routing, and connected-device coordination
- Documentation, labeling, and lifecycle planning
- Monitoring and operational visibility

## Structured cabling

- Copper and fiber pathway planning
- Cable installation, termination, testing, and labeling
- Rack organization and cleanup
- Documentation and handoff

## Conference room AV

- Display, audio, camera, and control-system planning
- Meeting-room installation and integration
- Network and cabling coordination
- Documentation, training, and support

Specific availability depends on location, project scope, technical fit, and scheduling.
Start with a [discovery call](https://codevelo.dev/book).
